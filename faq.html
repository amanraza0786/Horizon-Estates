Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with Horizon Estates.

What areas does Horizon Estates cover? +
We actively operate across Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Dwarka, Greater Noida and Faridabad, with select luxury listings in other NCR micro-markets.
Are all listed properties verified? +
Yes. Every property is physically inspected and its title documents are checked by our legal team before it is published on our platform.
Do you charge brokerage for buyers? +
Our fee structure is disclosed upfront during your first consultation — there are no hidden charges added later in the process.
Can you help me sell my property? +
Yes, we offer professional valuation, listing photography, marketing and buyer screening as part of our property selling service.
Do you assist with home loans? +
We work with leading banks and NBFCs to help you check eligibility and secure competitive interest rates on your home loan.
How long does a typical purchase take? +
For a ready-to-move property with clear title, the process from shortlist to registration usually takes 3–6 weeks.
Do you handle commercial leasing? +
Yes, we assist businesses with office, retail and warehouse leasing, including lease negotiation and documentation.
Can NRIs buy property through Horizon Estates? +
Yes, we regularly assist NRI clients with remote property search, virtual site visits and documentation support.
Do you offer property management services? +
We provide maintenance coordination, rent collection and tenant management for owners who don't reside near their property.
Is a site visit mandatory before booking? +
We strongly recommend it and arrange visits at your convenience — including weekend slots — before you finalize any property.
What documents are required to book a property? +
Typically PAN card, address proof, photographs and bank statements; our team shares an exact checklist based on your chosen property.
Do you help with property registration? +
Yes, our documentation team coordinates stamp duty payment, sale deed drafting and sub-registrar office formalities.
Can I rent a property through Horizon Estates? +
Yes, we list verified rental properties and handle tenant screening, agreements and move-in coordination.
Do you provide investment advice? +
Our advisors share market data on price trends, rental yield and upcoming infrastructure to help guide investment decisions.
How do I get in touch with an advisor? +
Call or WhatsApp us at +91 77008 20609, email amanraza84002@gmail.com, or fill out the contact form and we'll respond within a few hours.
