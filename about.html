About Horizon Estates

Fourteen years of helping Delhi NCR families and investors find property with confidence.

Our Story

Built On Trust, Grown Through Referrals

Horizon Estates began in 2010 as a small brokerage in Okhla, New Delhi, started with a simple belief: property decisions should never feel confusing or risky. What started with a handful of residential deals in South Delhi has grown into a full-service real estate brand covering Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Dwarka, Greater Noida and Faridabad.

Today, our team of advisors, legal experts and loan specialists work together on every transaction — because the majority of our new clients still come from referrals by families we've served before.
